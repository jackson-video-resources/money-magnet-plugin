# Money Magnet for Claude Code

Build your scorecard Money Magnet by chatting with Claude Code. The mod shows your seven steps beside the chat, puts every choice to you as options, and saves each answer to your workshop editor at lewiswjackson.com.

## Install

In Claude Code, one at a time:

```
/plugin marketplace add jackson-video-resources/money-magnet-plugin
/plugin install money-magnet@money-magnets
```

Paste your workshop key when it asks. It's on your workshop page: lewiswjackson.com/workshop/app

## Use

- `/magnet-next` does your next step
- `/magnet` shows your steps and what you've chosen
- `/magnet-check` checks your live link
- `/magnet-numbers` shows your visits, finishes, sign-ups and clicks
- `/magnet-video` sets up tracked links and QR codes for your next video

The steps panel draws in the terminal and the Claude desktop app. In other places (VS Code, Codex) the tools still work, without the panel.
